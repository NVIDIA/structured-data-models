Model Details
-------------

.. list-table::


   * - **Output**
     - 21 quantiles per target and time step named ``"{target}__q01"``, ``"{target}__q05"`` through ``"{target}__q95"``, ``"{target}__q99"``

   * - **Missing Values**
     - Handled natively

   * - **Pretraining Scope**
     - Up to 8,192 rows and 32 columns
